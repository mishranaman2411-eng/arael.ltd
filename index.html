OUR STORY

The ARAEL Story

ARAEL is an Indian streetwear label combining contemporary silhouettes with Indian folk-inspired visual motifs.

Drop 01 pairs substantial combed cotton fleece with traditional design references—Warli folk dancing figures, lotus forms, and prayer-arches.

Relaxed silhouettes, considered proportions, and clear shopping terms with nationwide COD and doorstep exchanges.
